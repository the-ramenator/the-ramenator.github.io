About

Ramenate is a free open source HTML5 online animation software application with limited functionality and incomprehensible code.

I got the idea for this a few years ago, when I was 10 and couldn't write a single line of code. I was really inspired by people like Alan Becker, and wanted to create my own animations. Problem was, I was a broke 10-year old, and couldn't figure out how to get animation programs to work on a chromebook. Years later, the idea of an online animator popped into my head, and well here we are.

Ramenate was made by The Ramenator, and you can find the source by pressing Ctrl+U.

I want to give a special thanks to Sean (from Sheeptester) for his amazing code and descriptions that I borrowed.

Help

Just your daily reminder that exporting doesn't work and probably never will. Until then, just draw random stuff and see what happens.
Here's what each of the buttons do.

  • Black box: color picker. click on it to pick a new color
  • 1st text box: FPS chooser. choose how fast you want your animation to be played/exported (to be made)
  • 2nd text box: Line Thickness. Choose how thick you want your drawing line to be.
  • Download: Export the animation (WIP)
  • Back button: Undo
  • Mop: Clear drawing area
  • Plus: Add the frame you were just drawing on to the list
  • Play: view the animation
  • Save: save an edited frame. edit a frame by pressing on it on the bottom bar. your current drawing area will be overtaken by the frame you clicked on.
  • Trash can with lines: delete an edited frame. Click on a frame on the bottom bar. Click this button to delete it.
  • Trash can with an x: Delete everything. All your frames. All your drawings. Everything.
